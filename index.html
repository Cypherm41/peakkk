Banners of Peakkk

A medieval real-time strategy game. Lead the Green kingdom against the Yellow realm.

Difficulty
  • Train builders at the Town Center and put them to work: chop trees, build farms, mine gold and stone.
  • Build houses before you hit the population cap, and storehouses next to forests and mines.
  • Raise an army from the Barracks, Archery Range and Stable. Swordsmen beat spearmen, spearmen beat cavalry, cavalry beat archers, archers beat swordsmen.
  • Destroy every Yellow building to win. Press F1 in game for all controls.

Victory!

Controls

Left click / dragSelect units or buildings (double click selects all of a type on screen)
Right clickMove · attack · gather · build / repair · set rally point for a selected building
WASD / arrows / screen edgeScroll the map (middle mouse drag also pans)
Mouse wheelZoom
Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C VCommand card hotkeys (the key shown on each button)
Shift + click / trainKeep placing buildings · train 5 units at once
A, then clickAttack-move: soldiers fight anything on the way
SStop (soldiers selected)
1–9 · Ctrl/Shift + 1–9Recall · assign a control group
. (period)Select next idle builder
SpaceJump to selection (or Town Center)
P · EscPause · cancel placement / deselect
